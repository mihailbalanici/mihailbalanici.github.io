Hi, and welcome! I’m Mihail Balanici, an electrical engineer working across the stack, from communications engineering to networking, data engineering and machine learning.

As a Research Associate at the Fraunhofer Heinrich Hertz Institute (HHI) in Berlin, I research and develop network automation solutions driven by telemetry data and machine learning. I build highly scalable telemetry frameworks and ETL and ML pipelines that enable the autonomous operation of optical networks, from indoor and access systems to transport and data center networks.

My path here led through vehicular (V2X) communications during my master's studies at Ulm University, and data center networking and cloud computing during my PhD at Kiel University. There I focused on ML-based traffic forecasting and optical circuit switching for hybrid electro-optical intra-data center networks.

As a firm believer in lifelong learning, I'm especially excited about the new frontiers of AI, such as agentic AI, building and integrating AI bots, and vibe coding, and I enjoy applying them in my own work.

Beyond technology, I read a lot about financial markets and investing, and I like to put that knowledge into practice. I see every euro as a small employee whose job is to help the capital grow. In my free time, you'll find me behind the camera, coding and building side projects for fun, on the tennis court, or tinkering with my watch collection.
